About Smart Money Notes

Smart Money Notes is an independent personal finance blog. Our goal is to explain budgeting, saving, investing basics and debt in plain English, without jargon or hype.

Our approach

Every article is researched from primary sources such as government agencies and fund providers, written to be practical, and updated when rules or numbers change. We don't promise quick riches, and we don't recommend specific stocks.
